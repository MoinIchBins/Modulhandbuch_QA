For **Dense Passage Retrieval (DPR)**, concentrate on the conceptual retrieval architecture: queries and passages are encoded separately into dense vectors and compared in representation space. Look for the contrast with sparse lexical retrieval and the efficiency benefits of a bi-encoder setup. You mainly need this as background for dense retrieval, not as evidence about your exact E5 model.
