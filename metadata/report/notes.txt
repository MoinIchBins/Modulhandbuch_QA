    ## Development Results: Top-k Selection

    For all three representations, **top-k = 1** produced the best development performance.

    * **E5** performed best overall, with an overall F1 of **0.617** and an answerable-question F1 of **0.744**.
    * **TF-IDF** ranked second, reaching an overall F1 of **0.551** and an answerable-question F1 of **0.665**.
    * **Sentence-BERT** performed substantially worse, with an overall F1 of **0.374** and an answerable-question F1 of **0.452**.

    The results indicate that retrieving only the highest-ranked chunk is already sufficient for most answerable questions. E5 provides the strongest ranking quality, while TF-IDF remains a competitive lexical baseline. Sentence-BERT is clearly weaker under the same top-k setting.

    ## Development Results: Threshold Selection

    The coarse threshold search identified interior optima for all three representations, indicating that the predefined search regions were appropriate and did not require expansion.

    * **E5** achieved the highest overall F1 (**0.494**) at a threshold of **0.8665**. It also showed strong abstention behavior on zero-gold questions (**0.824**), but lower answerable-question F1 (**0.426**).
    * **TF-IDF** achieved a slightly lower overall F1 (**0.475**) at **0.1162**, but performed best on answerable questions, with an F1 of **0.537** and recall of **0.661**. Its main weakness was poor zero-gold abstention (**0.176**).
    * **Sentence-BERT** performed worst overall, with an F1 of **0.351** at **0.6881**. Although its zero-gold abstention rate was high (**0.797**), its answerable-question F1 remained low (**0.259**).

    These results suggest that E5 currently provides the best balance between retrieval quality and abstention, whereas TF-IDF is strongest when a relevant answer exists but struggles to reject unanswerable questions.
    All are worse than the previous highest performance of top-k for E5

    The refined search confirmed the coarse-search optima for all three representations.

    * **E5** remained best overall at threshold **0.8665**, with overall F1 **0.494** and zero-gold abstention **0.824**.
    * **TF-IDF** improved slightly to overall F1 **0.487** at threshold **0.1277**. Its answerable-question F1 remained strongest (**0.537**), but abstention was still weak (**0.243**).
    * **Sentence-BERT** improved marginally to overall F1 **0.358** at threshold **0.7095**, with high abstention (**0.892**) but low answerable-question F1 (**0.248**).

    The refined search therefore did not change the overall ranking: **E5 > TF-IDF > Sentence-BERT** for threshold-based selection.

6.4 experiment
    method: see metadata/report/top_k_threshold_method.md
    ## Rank-Specific Threshold Search
    For each **representation × k** combination, a separate threshold interval is calculated from zero-gold development questions.

    Let (s_k(q)) be the score of the (k)-th ranked chunk. The search interval is
    [ L_k=P_{10}(s_k), \qquad U_k=P_{90}(s_1) ]
    [ \tau \in [L_k,U_k] ]

    This makes the threshold range specific to both the representation and the number of allowed chunks. The lower bound allows the (k)-th candidate to enter, while the upper bound covers strong abstention. Each interval is tested at 21 evenly spaced thresholds.
